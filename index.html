About

Hi, I’m Miwah.

I’m the founder and lead stylist behind Miwah Styles. With over two years of hands-on experience, I specialise in wig styling and revamping: restyling, restoring and transforming wigs into polished, confident looks tailored to each client. Beyond the studio, I host events with the same energy and attention to detail.


I also serve as the Parliamentary Representative for Year Two, FCMS, so leadership, communication and service shape how I work with every client. In a world where showing up is mandatory, beauty is non-negotiable and confidence is everything, Miwah Styles helps you become the best version of yourself, from undergraduates and corps members to broadcasters, managers and CEOs.
